Pink Cake

Home

Description

Something sweet and delicious, with a lot of heart.

Ingredients

  • Melon (1)
  • Wheat Flour (1)
  • Sugar (1)
  • Egg (1)

Steps

  1. Make in the kitchen with the ingredients
  2. Randomly appears in Krobus' shop for purchase
  3. If the player's character is male, after earning 120,000g, his mom will send a Pink Cake by mail
