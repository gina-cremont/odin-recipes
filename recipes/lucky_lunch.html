Lucky Lunch

Home

Description

A small, special meal that will increase your luck by 3.

Ingredients

  • Sea Cucumber (1)
  • Tortilla (1)
  • Blue Jazz (1)

Steps

  1. Make in the kitchen with the ingredients
  2. Purchase at the Desert Festival from the Calico Egg Merchant for 10 Calico Eggs
  3. Randomly appears in Krobus' shop for purchase
