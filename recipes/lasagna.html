Description

Here we will include a description of the lasagna. The recipe is 5 simple steps, and the entire process is under 10 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 Egg
  • 3 Pastas
  • 1/2 Cup of Milk

Steps

  1. Crack egg into a mixing bowl
  2. Whisk milk into the bowl with the egg
  3. Pour the mixture onto the first pasta piece, when fully mixed
  4. Continue for all three pasta pieces
